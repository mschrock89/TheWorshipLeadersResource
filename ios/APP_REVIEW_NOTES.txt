3. FUNCTIONS, AUDIENCE, AND VALUE
Wrshp Resource (Worship Resource) is a ministry operations app for worship leaders, staff, and volunteers.

Problem: church teams manage serving schedules, setlists, swaps, and communication across campuses using texts, spreadsheets, and disconnected tools.

Value: one signed-in hub to see when you serve, request or cover swaps, plan setlists, open songs/charts/audio, chat with your ministry, complete weekly DEVO, and get push notifications when plans change. Leaders can manage the roster, weekend flow, auditions, and church integrations.

Target audience: authorized church staff, worship/production volunteers, and audition candidates. Not a public consumer social app.

4. SETUP
No special setup. Use the demo account above. Core paths after login: Home → avatar menu → My Schedule, Team Directory, Song Library, Calendar, Chat. Games (Snake, Pong, Galager) are optional signed-in extras and are not the app’s purpose.

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES
• Supabase — email/password authentication, database, file storage, backend functions
• Apple Push Notification service (APNs) — native push
• Google (optional, leaders only) — Calendar, Sheets, and Gmail Audition Inbox. Google is not used for sign-in.
• Planning Center Online (optional, leaders only) — schedule and song sync
• Resend — transactional email (welcome, password reset, swap notices)
• Crossway ESV Bible API (api.esv.org) — licensed Bible text in the in-app reader
• OpenAI — optional staff tools (theme/song suggestions, chapter analysis, audio transcription for charts). Not required to review the app.
• YouTube — optional embedded rehearsal/song links
No payment processor, in-app purchases, ads, or tracking SDKs.

6. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. Features and content are the same in every region. The app is English and used by Experience Community Church in the United States. Campus differences are organizational (which campus a volunteer serves), not App Store territory variants.

7. REGULATED INDUSTRY / PROTECTED MATERIAL
Not a highly regulated industry (not healthcare, finance, gambling, or legal services).
