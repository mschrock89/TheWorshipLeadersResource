3. FUNCTIONS, AUDIENCE, AND VALUE
Ministry operations app for worship leaders, staff, and volunteers.
Problem: schedules, setlists, swaps, and communication were split across texts and spreadsheets.
Value: one signed-in hub for serving schedules, swaps, setlists, songs/charts/audio, chat, DEVO, and push updates. Leaders manage roster, weekend flow, auditions, and church integrations.
Audience: authorized church staff and volunteers. Not a public consumer social app.

4. SETUP

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES

No payments, IAP, ads, or tracking SDKs.
