A. APP PRIVACY — this is why they asked for ATT
========================================
You must have Account Holder or Admin.

1. App Store Connect → Wrshp Resource → App Privacy
2. Click Edit
3. Question: “Do you or your third-party partners use data from this app to track users?”
   Answer: NO
4. You still collect data for the app itself. Keep collection = Yes if asked.
5. For EVERY data type already listed, open it and set:
   • Used for Tracking Purposes = NO / unchecked
   • Used for Third-Party Advertising = NO
   • Used for App Functionality = YES
6. Especially fix these three (Apple named them):
   • Emails or Text Messages — tracking OFF
   • Precise Location — tracking OFF
   • Photos or Videos — tracking OFF
7. If a type is only used in the web/students product and not this iOS app, you can remove it from this app’s privacy labels.
8. Publish / Save the privacy answers before you resubmit.

Correct intent for this iOS app:
• Email Address — Account + App Functionality. Linked to user. Not tracking.
• Name — App Functionality. Linked to user. Not tracking.
• Phone Number — App Functionality. Linked to user. Not tracking.
• Photos or Videos — profile photo and chat attachments. App Functionality. Linked to user. Not tracking.
• Precise Location — optional campus attendance only, while that setting is on. App Functionality. Linked to user. Not tracking. Not used if the user never enables attendance.
• Other User Content (chat) — App Functionality. Not tracking.
• User ID — App Functionality. Not tracking.
• Product Interaction / Crash Data — only if you actually collect it (you do not use advertising SDKs).

Do NOT add App Tracking Transparency. The app does not track.
